Terms of Use

What MeoDeo is. MeoDeo is a free search engine for videos. It finds videos on other websites (YouTube, Dailymotion, PeerTube, the Internet Archive, NASA and others, plus the wider web through SearXNG) and shows them in a list with the website each one comes from.

MeoDeo doesn't host videos. MeoDeo never uploads, stores, copies or re-streams any video. A video plays in the original website's own official player (its "embed"), served by that website, with that website's ads where it has them, so creators and websites keep their views and income. If a website doesn't allow its videos to play elsewhere, MeoDeo respects that and only links to it.

Other websites' rules apply. When you watch a video, you're also using that website, under its own terms and privacy policy. MeoDeo doesn't control what other websites publish and isn't responsible for it.

Accounts and the feed. You can make a free MeoDeo account to share video links in the public feed. You must be 13 or older (or the minimum age for online services where you live, if higher). Keep your password safe; you're responsible for what's posted from your account. Your username and posts are public.

What you post. You keep any rights you have in your captions. By posting, you let MeoDeo show your post in the feed. Only post links you're allowed to share, and follow the Rules. We may hide or delete posts and close accounts that break these terms or the law, without notice.

Use MeoDeo fairly. Don't try to break, overload or scrape MeoDeo, get around its limits, or use it for anything illegal.

No warranty. MeoDeo is provided free and "as is". Results come from other websites and may be wrong, unavailable or change. To the extent the law allows, MeoDeo isn't liable for damage from using it or the websites it links to.

Changes. These terms may change; the date at the top shows the latest version.

Rules

These rules apply to everything shared on MeoDeo, and MeoDeo's search filters results the same way.

  • No sexual or adult content. No pornography, nudity or sexual content. Nothing sexual involving minors, ever: it's reported to the authorities.
  • No shock or violent content: no gore, real violence shown for shock, terrorism or self-harm promotion.
  • No hate or harassment: no attacks on people for who they are, no bullying, threats or sharing private information about someone.
  • No piracy: don't share films, shows, music or other work uploaded without the owner's permission, or sites that pirate them.
  • No spam, scams or malware.
  • Nothing illegal where you live or in the European Union.

How we enforce them. MeoDeo uses each website's safe search (YouTube SafeSearch, Dailymotion's family filter, PeerTube and Odysee without "sensitive" videos, SearXNG safe search) and its own filter that blocks adult, shock and piracy websites and words. Anyone can report a feed post with its Report button: a post reported by several different people is hidden automatically and reviewed. Rights holders can have a video removed straight away with a takedown request.

Privacy

Searching needs no account and MeoDeo doesn't build profiles, sell data or show its own ads.

  • Searches are sent to the video websites and to MeoDeo's search servers to get results. MeoDeo doesn't keep a history of them.
  • Accounts: we store your username, a scrambled (hashed) form of your password and your posts. Sign-in uses one cookie, only for keeping you signed in.
  • Reports and takedown requests: we store what you send. For reports and limits on repeated requests we keep a one-way hash of your IP address, never the address itself.
  • Settings (chosen websites, language, keys you add) stay in your own browser.
  • Other websites: when a video plays, the website it comes from (for example YouTube) may set its own cookies and show ads, under its own privacy policy.
  • Translation: only if you pick a language other than English, the page loads Google's website translator, and Google receives the page text to translate it (Google's privacy policy).
  • Hosting: MeoDeo runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver the site (Cloudflare's privacy policy).

You can delete your posts at any time. To delete your account or ask what we store about you, use the form below with "Privacy" as the request type.

Copyright & takedowns

MeoDeo respects creators' rights. It doesn't host any video; it shows videos through the official players of the websites that host them. To remove a video from the internet, contact the website that hosts it (for example YouTube's copyright form).

To remove a video or post from MeoDeo, send the request below. The link is removed from MeoDeo straight away: feed posts with it are hidden and it's left out of search results, then the request is reviewed. If you posted something that was removed and you believe it was a mistake, send a request of type "Other" explaining why (a counter-notice).

This form is MeoDeo's contact point for copyright notices (including DMCA-style notices) and for notices of illegal content under the EU Digital Services Act.
